      Field guide

      Last checked Nov 10, 2026

      How to use Plundr
      • Tap a chest every time you open one. Press and hold to take one off.
      • Tap New match when you drop into a new game.
      • Turn on Treasure Hour when Epic announces one — it switches itself off after 2 hours.
      • Start a session to see chests per hour. Your screen stays on while it runs.
      • Use ＋ Add or － Remove for chests you forgot to log.
      • Tap the ring to change your daily goal.
      • Add Plundr to your Home Screen so it opens like an app.
      What the token is
      • “Pirate Shop Access” is a rare chest drop in Battle Royale and Zero Build during the Treasure Hunters mini-season (Nov 1 – Dec 5, 2026).
      • Finding it gives two activations: one for 2026 and one for 2027.
      • Each activation makes V-Buck items in the Item Shop free to claim for 14 days, and what you claim is yours to keep.
      • The 2026 one must be activated by Dec 15, 2026, from the Item Shop.
      How to improve your real chances
      1. Open more chests per minute. Land at chest-dense spots and plan a loop.
      2. Play in a full squad. If anyone in the squad finds it, everyone gets it.
      3. Play during Treasure Hours. Epic announces them on the official Fortnite Status account; the token's odds are boosted 2×.
      4. Track your pace. Run a session to see chests per hour and tune your route.
      What's not included
      • Gifting items to others
      • Battle Pass levels and passes
      • Real-money packs and other non–V-Buck offers
      • More than one Sidekick
      ⚠️ Scam alert
      The token only drops from chests in-game. Any site, giveaway or account sale promising one is a scam — Epic has warned about accounts being sold on eBay.
